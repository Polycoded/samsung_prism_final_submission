# Meridian Hall conference guide

Fictional conference document prepared for the upload demonstration.
It is not organizer-provided material and is not part of the gated benchmark corpus.

## Capacity.1

The Meridian Hall capacity is 80 people in a seated workshop layout.

## Cancellation.1

The Meridian Hall cancellation policy allows a full refund when cancelled at least 14 days before the event.

## Catering.1

Meridian Hall catering options include vegetarian lunch and tea. Outside catering requires written approval.
